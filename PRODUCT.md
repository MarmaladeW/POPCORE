# Product

## Register

product

## Users

POPCORE is primarily used by store owners and managers to assign employee shifts across retail locations. Staff also use the schedule to understand their assigned shifts and availability. Managers need to work quickly on desktop while retaining a clear, touch-friendly view on mobile.

## Product Purpose

POPCORE brings product, stock, sales, restocking, staffing, and scheduling operations into one internal tool. The scheduling surface succeeds when a manager can immediately see who is assigned, identify coverage gaps, make changes confidently, and verify that every employee has been considered without losing the calendar itself.

## Brand Personality

Clean, efficient, and easy to use. The interface should feel dependable and direct, with visual detail serving fast operational decisions.

## Anti-references

- Desktop layouts merely shrunk onto a phone.
- Dense administrative panels that bury the primary calendar.
- Color-only status communication or tiny marks that cannot be identified.
- Decorative effects, excessive cards, or unfamiliar controls that slow routine work.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the schedule visible: secondary checks and notes must not crowd out the calendar.
2. Make people recognizable: preserve each employee's assigned color and pair it with a readable name or abbreviation.
3. Adapt structure, not just size: mobile should prioritize glanceable assignments and comfortable touch targets.
4. Surface exceptions first: unresolved staffing and coverage issues deserve attention; completed items should become visually quiet.
5. Prefer familiar, reversible controls so managers can assign, check, and correct shifts quickly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, and touch targets of roughly 44 pixels on coarse pointers. Never rely on color alone: employee and coverage colors must be paired with names, initials, icons, or text. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep schedule actions usable without hover.
