# Native POPCORE checkout

The `/checkout` workspace is the 买单 entry from the four-option staff home at `/`. It connects per-order cashier work, payment photos and the existing sale/stock/closing flow. Home, Checkout and Schedule remain directly accessible on mobile; Order history and the previous Today workspace (Store tasks) remain in navigation. See [Staff home](staff-home.md) for the September 16 entrance and event-recording flow.

## Use

The queue uses today's permitted locations and identifies each order by register (when supplied), amount, items and reference. The cashier selects the customer's order; background refresh never switches that selection. Each order displays its original individual POPCORE cashier. The application does not use the shared Clover intern login as a personal identity.

A manual order is paid the same way as a Clover order (see [Clover checkout](clover-checkout.md)), with the same prices from `popcore_app/clover_pricing.py`. Each tender button shows its price. Card pays the order total. Cash, E-transfer, WeChat Pay and Alipay get the cash discount. Up to $15 it is the nearest whole dollar to 2.5% off, ties down and always below the total (for example, $7.12 becomes $7). Above $15 it takes at least $1 off, or 2.5% when that is more, and rounds down to the dollar: $20.00 becomes $19, $22.59 becomes $21, $41.34 becomes $40 and $99.14 becomes $96 (owner, 2026-10-20). Totals just under a whole dollar therefore get almost $2 off; for example, $22.99 becomes $21. **Split payment** with card floors totals of at least $10 to the whole dollar, and smaller totals stay exact; for example, $7.90 stays $7.90 and $28.31 becomes $28.00. A split without card keeps the cash price. An agreed discount entered on the order keeps rounding only. Card is recorded in POPCORE after it is taken on the card machine, because a manual order has no Clover order. The cashier taps the tender, collects, and taps **Received**. The server prices every receipt and refuses one whose price changed (`price_changed`). **Take back** removes a payment recorded by mistake, with a reason, until the sale is recorded.

WeChat Pay and Alipay show the CNY amount to charge at the rate set in Settings. Without one, they use that day's reference rate (European Central Bank, with a second source as backup), looked up at most once an hour and labelled with its source and date. Each WeChat Pay or Alipay payment keeps the yuan charged and the rate used at that moment, so a later rate never changes it. The server works the yuan out again and refuses a payment whose yuan do not match its rate. The order's payments, its payment photo, order history cards and the sale's payment review show the yuan beside the dollars, for example `¥209.12 charged at 1 CAD = 4.7528 CNY`. Payments recorded before 2026-10-20 have no yuan.

Staff choose the actual payment method. E-transfer, WeChat Pay and Alipay ask for the payment photo next: **Take photo** opens the camera, and **Upload photo** chooses an existing picture such as a screenshot. Use photo uploads immediately to that exact order and payment. Cash and card require no photo. A photo can arrive after payment and does not verify receipt. Ambiguous writes retain their original request key and body for Retry; switching stores, orders or history dates is disabled until resolved. Access denial clears sensitive details and drafts.

The cashier or an authorized manager explicitly records money actually received. Split payments remain separate. Once the received total matches the amount due, the sale is recorded with its stock movement, and the reduction is the difference from the original total. Failed finalization retains received money and offers retry; it never records the receipt again. Managers can expand the secondary cancel/refund section to abandon a partially paid checkout and record actual refunds. Entirely unpaid checkouts can be cancelled by their authorized cashier.

Start manual checkout opens `/checkout/new` for an authorized live store and creates native orders using exact catalog items and captured prices/tax/discount. The order reference is optional: a phone order, or an item another store is sending, has no Clover order, so POPCORE numbers it per store and day (for example `MK-20261020-001`). Such a sale belongs to the store the item leaves from, on the day it is picked up. The tax total fills itself in as 13% HST of the item prices, rounded to the cent as Clover rounds, and can be changed by hand; the form shows the final amount the customer pays. Card and cash for such orders are taken at pickup (store rule, 2026-10-20). Record completed sale remains a separate intake action. At the store linked to Clover, Clover orders arrive by themselves and are paid as described in [Clover checkout](clover-checkout.md); the manual form stays the fallback when Clover is not connected. Captured totals/items are immutable; cancel an entirely unpaid native checkout and create a replacement to correct them. Unpaid orders do not reserve stock. An inventory shortage preserves the financial sale with pending allocation for manager resolution.

## Access

- Staff: live orders at locations with an assigned shift today, for the entire Toronto calendar day. Availability alone grants no access. Order history contains only their own orders and stays available off duty; on shift it also shows that day's sales the Clover register recorded at that location.
- Managers: live orders and staff history only at their locations assigned today, including when looking at historical dates.
- Admin: all active locations and historical dates for reads, without requiring a Schedule profile or shift. Existing inventory and financial write guards still apply.
- These checks run on the server, including linked sale/payment/evidence/report/closing paths and transactional rechecks before replay or mutation. Viewing an order never changes its cashier. Assigned photo helpers cannot use assignment to read another employee's completed history.

## Boundaries

- Clover orders follow [Clover checkout](clover-checkout.md), through the separate connector service and its exact merchant, order and payment identities. The application holds no Clover OAuth credentials, simulator actor picker, public fake webhook, or provider verification bypass. The manual flow here stays for stores without Clover and for times Clover is not connected.
- Clover works out the tax on its own orders, and POPCORE takes Clover's total as it is. On a manual order the 13% HST is only the starting value of the tax field, not a rule that every item is taxed. It rounds the order's tax half up to the cent, which matched all five sandbox Clover orders on 2026-10-20.
- A partially collected checkout requires a scoped manager to mark it abandoned, then record actual refunds against the original payments. Full repayment automatically cancels it. Original receipts and evidence remain intact; cancellation creates no sale and moves no stock. Ordinary refunds and separate physical returns remain available after a completed sale.
- The visible queue refreshes every 15 seconds and on window focus, pausing during pending writes. It lists open orders, including older unresolved orders, in pages of 100. History supports date filtering and pagination, a payment-method filter, and a keyword search over item names (including Chinese names), the order reference or Clover order ID, and the order note. Each history card shows the amount actually received and how it was paid, with the original total when that differs. At the Clover store the queue refreshes every second while visible, and the server reads Clover at most once a second across all screens.
- Photo files use the existing private normalized image storage. Staged and cancelled-attempt images are included in backup/restore; only completed-attempt images attach to sale payments. A photo never verifies payment. Existing review permissions remain in force.
- The checkout implementation is included on `codex/staff-home`. It has not been deployed or built into the committed production bundle.

## Abandoned checkout refund API

This backend flow records refunds already made outside POPCORE. It does not send money or call Clover. Secondary manager controls in the checkout screen submit this flow.

1. `POST /api/checkouts/{id}/abandon` with `expected_version` and a nonempty `reason`. Requires a manager with inventory access to the checkout store. It cancels pending attempts and stops further collection/finalization; original completed receipts remain unchanged. Entirely unpaid orders still use `cancel`.
2. `POST /api/checkouts/{id}/refund` with the latest `expected_version`, original completed `attempt_id`, positive integer `amount_cents`, actual `business_date`, a store-unique refund `reference` (1–120 characters), `reason`, and `confirmed_received_and_refunded: true`. The manager must check both the original receipt and the refund actually made. The tender is inherited from that original attempt. The refund date must be on or after the original checkout business date and no later than today in Toronto.
3. Both actions require an `Idempotency-Key`. Reuse the same key and identical body for a retry. A different refund needs a new key and external reference. Refunds cannot exceed the unrefunded amount of their exact payment attempt. The checkout cancels automatically after all received money has been refunded.

Checkout detail returns abandonment reason/actor/time, refund history, `refunded_cents`, `refund_due_cents`, each attempt's `refundable_cents`, and manager `can_refund`. `received_cents` remains the gross receipt history; `remaining_cents` remains the original purchase balance. Use `refund_due_cents` for abandoned checkout work. Existing attempt evidence can be added before final cancellation and remains readable afterward and through backup/restore.

Receipts retain the checkout business date; refunds use their actual refund date. Manager confirmation verifies that original receipt for closing. Cash drawer totals include only cash; tender reports retain gross receipts and refunds separately. An unfinished abandonment blocks closing on its original date and any date with refunds against it. Changes invalidate affected closing source tokens and cash counts; entries recorded after closing create late adjustments while the signed snapshot remains unchanged. A refund-day float shortage still requires replenishment through the existing cash workflow before closing.
